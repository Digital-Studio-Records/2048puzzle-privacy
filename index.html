2048 Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Developer: Digital Studio Records

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game 2048 Puzzle (the "App"), published by Digital Studio Records ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. In short: 2048 Puzzle does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and does not access your camera or microphone.

The App does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking tools, and it requests no special Android permissions.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following game data only on your own device:

  • Games in progress for Classic and Special Mode, including the board, score, moves and play time, so you can continue where you left off
  • Best scores and game statistics for Classic, Special Mode and Time Attack
  • Settings such as theme, sound and vibration preferences

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, access it or recover it.

3. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use, sell, rent or share it for any purpose, including advertising.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by either:

  • Uninstalling 2048 Puzzle, or
  • Opening your device's Settings → Apps → 2048 Puzzle → Storage → Clear data

Deleting this data permanently resets your progress, scores and statistics, and it cannot be restored. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

5. Security

Your game data is stored using your device's standard app storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's normal security protections. No data is transmitted over a network.

6. Children's privacy

2048 Puzzle is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to its use. Because 2048 Puzzle collects no personal information, we hold no such data. You can delete all game data stored on your device at any time as described in Section 4.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the App begins collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
